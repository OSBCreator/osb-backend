OSB · Data & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Ref · OSB/LEGAL/PP/001/2026 Effective · 11 June 2026 Last updated · June 2026

Online Security Bureau (OSB) exists to protect people from behavioural exploitation. Protecting your data is part of that mission, not separate from it. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and the control you keep over it. Privacy by Design is a founding principle of OSB — every tool we build is designed to collect the minimum it needs and nothing more.

01Who we are

Online Security Bureau ("OSB", "we", "us") is the data controller responsible for the information described in this policy. OSB is a registered entity (CAC Reg. No. 3648862) and processes personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), under the supervisory authority of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). Where we process the data of individuals located in the European Union or United Kingdom, we also align our practices with the EU GDPR and UK GDPR.

03Our privacy principles

  • Data minimisation. We collect only what a feature genuinely needs to work.
  • Consent first. Where we process information you provide through a tool, we ask for clear consent before we do.
  • No selling, ever. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No advertising, no tracking. We do not run advertising and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Deletion on request. You can ask us to delete data we hold about you at any time.

04What we collect, and why

RSEI Scoring Tool

When you use our scoring tool, you answer a short set of behavioural questions so the tool can generate a private read of your situation. This processing happens only after you give consent. We do not store your IP address, and we do not log the content of your answers against your identity. Inputs are sanitised before processing. We retain only anonymous, aggregate outcomes (for example, how many assessments fell into each risk band) to understand patterns and improve the model — and these aggregates are released only above a minimum threshold so no single person can be identified from them.

Contact & support

If you write to us — through a contact form or by email — we receive your name, email address and your message, and we use them solely to respond to you and to provide support. If you reach out through OSB Solace, a real person responds; we treat what you share with the care the subject deserves and never use it for anything other than helping you.

Community

If you join the OSB intelligence community, we collect a username and email address solely to set up and secure your place. Sign-ups are submitted through our form provider (Formspree) and reach us so we can create your account. The community is moderated, has no private inbox, and is built so that no unnecessary personal data is stored. You can request deletion at any time.

Intelligence briefings

If you subscribe to OSB briefings, you give us your email address — submitted through our form provider (Formspree) — and we use it only to send those briefings. You can unsubscribe at any time, and we never repurpose that address.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to operate the site and functional cookies to remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Details are set out in our Cookie Policy.

Security & abuse prevention

Our infrastructure keeps minimal technical records needed to keep the service available, secure, and free from abuse (such as rate-limiting automated attacks). These are kept only as long as needed for that purpose and are not used to profile you.

05Our legal bases

Under the NDPA and GDPR we rely on: your consent (for the scoring tool, community sign-up and briefings); the performance of a service you have asked for (responding to your enquiry); and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and operational, balanced against your rights.

06How your data is stored and protected

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and held in access-controlled systems. OSB runs on a small number of reputable infrastructure providers who act as our processors under contract and may only handle data on our instructions: Cloudflare (website hosting, content delivery and DNS), Supabase (database and serverless backend functions), Formspree (processing of community sign-up and newsletter form submissions), Flutterwave (payment processing), and Microsoft 365 (business email correspondence). None of them is authorised to use your data for their own purposes.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

07How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law. Anonymous, aggregate scoring outcomes are retained for research and model improvement. Contact and account data are deleted on request, and otherwise reviewed periodically and removed when no longer needed.

08Your rights

Under the NDPA, and the GDPR where it applies, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Have inaccurate data corrected;
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Receive your data in a portable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with the NDPC (or your local supervisory authority).

To exercise any of these, contact our DPO at contact@onlinesecuritybureau.com. We respond within the timeframes required by law.

09International transfers

OSB serves people across the world, so data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where data is transferred internationally, we take steps to ensure it remains protected to the standards described in this policy.

10Vulnerable people & minors

OSB supports people who have been targeted by exploitation, and we hold that responsibility seriously. Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child's data has reached us, contact our DPO and we will remove it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as OSB evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, communicated directly. The reference code (OSB/LEGAL/PP/001/2026) versions this document.

12Contact

For any privacy question or request, reach our Data Protection Officer at contact@onlinesecuritybureau.com, or through our contact page.
