Cookie Policy

Last updated · June 2026 · Companion to our Privacy Policy (OSB/LEGAL/PP/001/2026)

OSB uses cookies sparingly, and only to make the site work and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we never sell your data.

01 What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They let a site remember things between pages and visits — for example, that you've already accepted this notice. Cookies cannot run programs or carry viruses.

02 The cookies we use

TypePurposeCan you turn it off?
EssentialRequired for the site to function — security, load balancing, and remembering your cookie choice.These are necessary for the site to work, so they can't be switched off, but they store no marketing data.
FunctionalRemember your preferences so your experience is smoother on return visits.Yes — you can block these in your browser without breaking core functionality.
Advertising / trackingNone. OSB does not run advertising and does not track you across other websites.Not applicable — we don't use them.

OSB products carry no advertising and no third-party tracking pixels. Your visit isn't a product we sell.

03 Managing cookies

You can accept or limit cookies through the notice shown on your first visit, and you can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Most browsers let you delete existing cookies and refuse new ones; doing so won't stop you reading the site, though some preferences may not be remembered.

04 Changes & contact

If our use of cookies changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date. For any question, contact our Data Protection Officer at contact@onlinesecuritybureau.com. For the full picture of how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy.
