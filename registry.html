How the registry
stays honest.

No platform can add itself to this registry. Every listing is awarded by OSB following a completed assessment. Every listing is verifiable.

01

OSB-Awarded Only

Certification cannot be purchased or self-applied. It is awarded exclusively by OSB. No exceptions.

02

Versioned & Dated

Every certification carries the assessment date and RSEI version it was assessed against. The record is permanent and traceable.

03

Publicly Verifiable

Any user, journalist, regulator or researcher can check this registry at any time. A platform claiming certification not listed here is making a false claim.
