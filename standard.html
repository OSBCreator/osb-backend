The RSEI Standard™

Behavioural Intelligence Framework for Social Engineering Fraud — Version 2.0 · Reference · OSB/STD/RSEI/2.0 · Version · 2.0 · Current · Issued By · OSB · Status

Active Standard

Section 01

Introduction to The RSEI Standard™

The RSEI Standard™ is a structured behavioural threat detection and response framework developed specifically for the identification, classification and intervention of social engineering fraud. It was developed by OSB and is maintained as a living standard under active field research.

social engineering fraud operates through identifiable, repeatable behavioural patterns. Prior to the RSEI Standard™, no structured framework existed to score, classify and document these patterns in a consistent, evidence-based manner.

Foundational Principle

Social engineering fraud has a name. It has a score. The RSEI Standard™ is the instrument through which both are formally established and applied.

Section 02

Scope & Application

Individual Assessment: Any individual may apply the RSEI scoring methodology to their situation. The OSB Score Tool implements this directly and is available free of charge.

Platform Certification: Dating and social platforms may apply for formal RSEI Certification — confirming that the platform has been assessed and has demonstrated adequate structural safeguards against social engineering fraud behaviour.

Research & Documentation: The RSEI Standard™ provides the framework against which OSB documents field intelligence notes (IFN series). These notes form the evidence base from which the standard is developed and updated.

Section 03

The Four Pillars

Every documented social engineering case exhibits behaviour traceable to one or more of these pillars. They are derived from field case analysis and updated as new intelligence is documented.

CodePillarDefinitionScore
RRelationship AccelerationArtificial acceleration of emotional intimacy, dependency and commitment. Includes premature declarations of love, manufactured urgency and engineered exclusivity.1–5
SScript ConsistencyRehearsed, constructed persona — fabricated occupation, location, backstory and financial credibility props designed to appear independently verifiable.1–5
EEmotional ManipulationExploitation of trust, guilt, love and fear. E ≥ 4 triggers mandatory victim support referral regardless of financial loss status.1–5
IIncremental ExtractionEscalating financial requests — beginning small, establishing precedent, scaling to significant transfer. Methods include gift cards, wire transfer, cryptocurrency and mule recruitment.1–5

Section 04

Scoring Methodology

Each pillar is scored 1–5. The four scores are summed to produce the RSEI Core Score, ranging from 4 (minimum) to 20 (maximum).

Formula

RSEI Core Score = R + S + E + I · Range: 4–20
ESI (Escalation & Severity Indicator): 1–5 supplementary velocity measure

Section 05

Risk Classification

ScoreClassificationProtocol

Section 06

Independent Triggers

E Pillar Independent Trigger — Active at E ≥ 4

When E ≥ 4, mandatory victim support referral is activated — regardless of Core Score and regardless of whether any financial loss has occurred. Emotional harm is real harm. Established through field case IFN-002. Unique to the RSEI Standard™.

Section 07

Platform Certification

RSEI Certification is awarded exclusively by OSB following a completed assessment against the RSEI Standard™ v2.0. Certification confirms platform safeguards are commensurate with the four RSEI pillars. See certification.html for full details.

Section 08

The Public Registry

Only platforms listed on the registry hold RSEI Certification. No platform can claim RSEI Certification without appearing on the registry. Any claim not supported by a listing should be treated as unverified and reported to OSB. See registry.html.

Section 09

Issuing Authority

The RSEI Standard™ is developed and maintained by OSB (OSB). Created by John Dee, Founder and Director. OSB is the sole issuing authority for RSEI Certification.
