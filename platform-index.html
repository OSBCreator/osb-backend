Platform Safety Index

How we assess
platform safety.

Romance fraudsters, catfishers, investment scammers and sextortion operators find their targets on dating and social platforms. The OSB Platform Safety Index measures what each platform does to prevent it — using a published method, dated evidence and a right of reply.

Why This Matters

Platforms create the conditions.
OSB measures the responsibility.

Social engineering operators do not operate in a vacuum. They operate on platforms that profit from user engagement — including engagement manufactured by fraudsters across every typology.

The OSB Platform Safety Index is a systematic assessment of dating and social platforms on their anti-social-engineering measures. Each platform is assessed on five criteria and given an OSB Safety Score out of 100.

A rating is published only when every finding is sourced and dated, and the platform has been offered a right of reply. The index is reviewed annually.

OSB Assessment Criteria · 01

Identity Verification

Does the platform require users to verify who they are before contacting others?

02

AI Fraud Detection

Does the platform use automated tools to detect social engineering behaviour?

03

Abuse Reporting

How fast and effective is response to fraud reports?

04

Scam-Specific Tools

Are there tools specifically built to detect and stop social engineering fraud?

05

User Education

Does the platform actively warn users about social engineering risk?

Grading: A (80–100) · B (65–79) · C (50–64) · D (35–49) · F (0–34)

OSB · 2026 Assessment Cycle

2026 ratings are in preparation.

Platform-by-platform ratings will be published here once each assessment meets the standard below. Until then, OSB publishes its method and the techniques documented in its own case records.

Sourced

Every finding cites a public source, a platform statement or an OSB case record.

Dated

Every rating states the date it was assessed. Platforms change, and so will the scores.

Right of reply

Each platform sees its findings before publication and may respond. Responses are published with the rating.

Independent

Ratings are unsponsored and not for sale. Certification fees never influence the index, and certification is not one of its criteria.

Corrections and platform responses: contact@onlinesecuritybureau.com
