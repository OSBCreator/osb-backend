04

The Proxy Caller Exploit — documented first globally by OSB

IFN-001: The scammer held stolen account credentials — real money, real account, no freeze. Unable to call the bank themselves, they groomed the victim to make the call instead. Armed with scripted questions, the victim confirmed a staggering real balance — and unknowingly cleared the path for stolen funds to move. Their legitimacy was the weapon. OSB documented this first globally.
