02 · How a scam builds · Illustrative, not a single case

Sixty days. Seven moves.

Step through it from the target’s side. The operator lines are quoted from OSB dispatches. The pattern is drawn from the RSEI pillars and OSB case records; not every case follows every step.

  1. Day 1 · First contact on a dating app or social network
  2. Day 5 · Daily contact, love declared, a future planned
  3. Day 12 · The chat moves to WhatsAppLet’s talk on WhatsApp.The platform can no longer see this conversation
  4. Day 19 · A first small ask
  5. Day 27 · A crisis with a clock on itMy equipment broke down, I need to transfer money urgently, but I cannot access my account from here.
  6. Day 38 · The ask, framed as a loanI will pay you back when I return.
  7. Day 52 · Recruited to move moneyHelp me move something small.
