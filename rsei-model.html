The Framework

The RSEI Model™
Version 2.0

A structured behavioural scoring system built specifically for social engineering fraud. Four pillars. One score. Complete clarity.

What the RSEI Model™ does.

Social engineering fraudsters follow patterns. Those patterns are professional, rehearsed and consistent across thousands of cases globally. The RSEI Model™ names those patterns, scores them, and gives every person a clear answer.

Developed through field research, documented case analysis and direct intervention in active scam situations since 2021.

R

Pillar 01

Relationship Acceleration

1—5

Scammers build emotional dependency at a speed no legitimate relationship can sustain. Within days — sometimes hours — they express deep love, plan a future, call you their soulmate. This acceleration is not romance. It is engineering.

Love-bombing · Soulmate language · Future-faking · Isolation from support · Intensity escalation

S

Pillar 02

Script Consistency

1—5

Every social engineering fraudster operates from a script. The details are rehearsed, consistent and designed to be verifiable enough to feel real but untraceable enough to avoid exposure. Common profiles: military officer, oil rig engineer, widowed doctor, international contractor.

Military overseas · Oil rig / engineering · Facade bank page · Proxy caller exploit · Real bank details as props

E

Pillar 03

Emotional Manipulation

1—5

Scammers build emotional dependency and then weaponise it. Techniques range from guilt induction and manufactured crisis to extreme emotional distress language including suicidal ideation.

E Pillar Independent Trigger — Active at E ≥ 4

When E score reaches 4 or above, OSB mandates a victim support referral regardless of total Core Score and regardless of whether any financial loss has occurred. Emotional harm is real harm.

Guilt induction · Medical emergencies · Suicidal ideation script · Manufactured urgency · Anger on refusal

I

Pillar 04

Incremental Extraction

1—5

Scammers rarely ask for large sums immediately. They start small and escalate. The I pillar also covers money mule recruitment — where the victim is asked to receive, handle or forward money — exposing them to criminal liability regardless of intent.

Gift card requests · Cryptocurrency · Wire transfers · Money mule recruitment · Account access requests · Scoring System

Phase II · In Technical Development

RSEI Security Tool™

The RSEI Model™ scores behaviour. The RSEI Security Tool enforces it. Phase II is a native OS-level application that detects, fingerprints, and permanently blocks social engineering operators at the infrastructure layer — across every platform they touch.

Every confirmed operator generates a Phantom ID — a composite hardware fingerprint that persists across device resets, new accounts, and new SIMs. The Operator Intelligence Network (OIN) propagates confirmed Phantom IDs across all integrated platforms simultaneously.
