The 30-Day Companion

A daily walk back to yourself, after romance fraud

This is not a course to finish or a test to pass. It is a companion for the hardest weeks. One short reading a day, in the morning if you can. Some days will land; some won't — that's allowed. Some people feel lighter long before day 30, and that is not cheating. Healing is not a straight line, and it is not a race.

This guide walks beside the people who can help you — a trusted friend, a counsellor, a support group, your doctor. It does not replace them.

If a day stirs something up and you need to talk, you can reach OSB. We have sat with many people exactly where you are. Write to us via our contact page and a real person who understands will answer. You never have to sit alone with what a reading brings up.

And if you ever feel you might harm yourself, please reach out right now — to a crisis line, emergency services, or someone near you. That help is built for that moment, and it is faster than waiting on an email. You matter. Reaching out is strength, not weakness.

You are not alone. There are millions of people, right now, walking the same road you are.
