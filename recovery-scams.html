Recovery Scams:
Don't Pay Twice

John Dee · OSB · February 12, 2026 · OSB Intelligence Dispatch · Scam Watch

In 2025, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission reported that imposter and advance-fee scams, a category that includes recovery scams, accounted for nearly $3 billion in reported losses.

🎯 Reality Check:

If you've lost money to a scam, your funds are not sitting somewhere waiting to be recovered.

In most cases, the money is already gone — quickly withdrawn, transferred, or spent.

Recovery "agents" who promise: "We can recover your funds" are scammers looking to exploit you again.

✅ Key Takeaway:

Never pay anyone to recover your lost money.

If you are a victim, report the incident through the FBI's IC3 complaint portal.

Japanese Proverb:

"Fool me once, shame on you. Fool me twice, shame on me."
