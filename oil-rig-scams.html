Oil Rig Scams:
The Romance Trap You Didn't See Coming

John Dee · OSB · August 2025 · OSB Intelligence Dispatch · Scam Information and help

Most people know about romance scams — but did you know there are over 20 types of them?
One of the most manipulative is the Oil Rig Scam.

🚩 Here's how it works:

  • You meet someone online — charming, professional, and claims to be an engineer on an offshore oil rig (often with Shell or another big company).
  • They say they're stationed in a remote location and can't come home yet.
  • After a period of love bombing, they tell you there's a crisis: "My equipment broke down, I need to transfer money urgently, but I can't access my account from here."
  • They give you a fake bank login and ask you to transfer money for them.
  • Suddenly, the account gets "frozen".
  • Then they beg you to cover the cost upfront — "I'll pay you back when I return!"

💸 But here's the truth:

  • ❌ There is NO oil rig
  • ❌ NO broken equipment
  • ❌ NO real bank account
  • ❌ Just an emotional setup to steal your money

🛑 Sounds familiar? You're not alone — and you're not foolish.

Scammers use emotions as weapons. But now you know the trick.

"Trust — but VERIFY."
