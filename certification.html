What Certification Means

An independent assessment.
On public record.

When a platform carries the RSEI Certified™ mark, it shows that the platform was assessed against the RSEI Standard™ v2.0 and met its requirements for detecting and responding to social engineering fraud, on the date recorded in the registry.

01

Assessed

Independently assessed

Assessed against the RSEI Model™ v2.0 — a behavioural threat detection and response framework for social engineering fraud. Certification records that your platform met the standard on the date it was assessed.

02

User Confidence

Users can see what was assessed

The registry shows what was assessed, against which version of the standard, and when. Trust rests on a public record rather than a marketing claim.

03

Trust Signal

Public trust mark

The RSEI Certified™ mark is a public signal. Users comparing platforms can see, before they sign up, that yours has been assessed against the standard.

The Certification Signal

The first thing a user
should look for.

When an individual wants to join a dating or social platform, the first question they should ask is: is this platform RSEI Certified?

If it is, they can see when it was assessed and against which version of the standard. No certification can guarantee that a platform is free of fraud. It shows that the platform's defences have been independently examined. Certification fees never influence the OSB Platform Safety Index, and certification status is not one of its criteria.

The Process

Four steps.
One standard.

Every platform is assessed against the RSEI Model™ v2.0 — the same detection and response system behind OSB’s behavioural social engineering fraud framework.

01

Enquiry & Briefing

Platform contacts OSB. We establish scope, platform type and current safety infrastructure. No commitment required.

02

RSEI Assessment

OSB conducts a structured assessment against all four RSEI pillars. Behavioural risk exposure is mapped and documented.

03

Framework Alignment

Where gaps are found, OSB works with the platform to implement framework-aligned safeguards. Collaborative, not punitive.

04

Certification Awarded

RSEI Certified™ status awarded. The platform is listed on the public registry — permanently, on record.
