OSB · Trust & Security

Trust & Security

Ref · OSB/SEC/TSC/001/2026 · Effective · September 2026 · Review cycle · Quarterly

OSB asks people in distress to tell us what happened to them. That imposes an obligation to be explicit about how the platform is built, what we hold, who can reach it, and what we do not do. This page states that plainly, including where our controls are limited.

01 Who is accountable

Security and data protection at OSB sit with the same office, deliberately. Separating them tends to produce systems that are compliant on paper and weak in practice.

Accountable Office

Office of the Data Protection Officer

OSB's appointed Data Protection Officer holds the IIM — Certified Data Protection Officer credential, issued under the authority of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), and is verifiable with the issuing body using the certificate number below. The same office is responsible for platform security architecture and incident response.

02 Architecture

OSB's public surface is served as static files. All server-side logic runs in managed, isolated functions rather than on servers OSB operates or patches. The attack surface is deliberately narrow: a small number of defined endpoints, no general-purpose application server, and no long-lived credentials in anything delivered to a browser.

Where state is held, it is held deliberately and disclosed. Live chat maintains a conversation record so an operator can continue supporting the same person; those records are covered by the retention policy in section 03. Installed and cached versions of the site store content on the user's own device to work offline. Neither is incidental — both are documented here rather than left implicit.

03 Controls in place

Stated as implemented, not as aspiration.

Transport

TLS enforced Active

All traffic served over HTTPS. No mixed content.

Database access

Deny by default Active

Row Level Security enabled with no permissive policy. Only the server-side service role can read or write.

Origin control

CORS restricted Active

API accepts requests only from the OSB domain.

Abuse control

Server-side rate limiting Active

Per-endpoint limits enforced in the database, not the browser.

Input handling

Sanitised & bounded Active

All submitted fields are trimmed and length-limited server-side before storage.

Secrets

Never in source Active

Credentials held in the platform secret store. No keys in the repository or in client code.

Administrative access

Server-validated Active

Admin authentication is verified server-side against a stored secret. No credential exists in client-side code.

Retention

Automated deletion Active

Live chat transcripts are deleted automatically twelve months after a conversation closes.

Monitoring

24/7 case response Active

Inbound cases and live chat are monitored continuously by the OSB response team.

Continuous review

Quarterly posture review Active

Controls and configuration reviewed each quarter against current threat activity.

04 What OSB does not do

Absence of a capability is itself a control.

  • No accounts required to use OSB. The assessment, the community and live chat can all be used without registering. No password is collected, so none can be leaked, and there is no account-takeover surface. This is a deliberate design decision: people affected by this kind of fraud frequently will not create an account to ask for help, and requiring one would exclude those who most need it.
  • No advertising or tracking. No advertising networks, no behavioural trackers, no data sold or shared for marketing.
  • No identity verification of third parties. OSB does not confirm whether a named individual is or is not a fraud operator, and does not publish accusations against individuals.
  • No card data. Payment details are handled entirely by the payment provider.
  • No unnecessary identifiers. The assessment can be completed without a name. Live chat requires no email address.

05 Responsible disclosure

If you find a vulnerability in OSB's platform, report it and it will be treated seriously and without hostility.

Report to: contact@onlinesecuritybureau.com with the subject line SECURITY.

Our commitment: acknowledgement within 72 hours, an assessment within 14 days, and credit in this page's change log if you wish to be named.

What we ask: give us reasonable time before public disclosure, do not access, alter or exfiltrate data belonging to other people, and do not run automated testing that would degrade service for victims relying on it.

OSB will not pursue legal action against researchers acting in good faith under these terms. There is currently no paid bounty programme.

06 Independence and funding

OSB publishes assessments and intelligence about platforms and operators. That work is only credible if the funding behind it is visible.

  • OSB is a registered entity, self-funded and independently operated.
  • No platform, dating service or social network has paid OSB to be assessed, rated or omitted from an assessment.
  • Published findings are not shared with an assessed party for approval before publication.
  • Where a commercial relationship exists with any organisation referenced in OSB output, it will be disclosed within that output.

07 Analytical integrity

OSB's core methodological rule: figures that cannot be sourced are not published.

  • Quantified claims OSB cannot independently support are marked [VERIFY] and are not presented as established fact.
  • Reference ranges for RSEI scoring remain marked as unvalidated until a cohort of confirmed-outcome cases is large enough to support them.
  • Third-party statistics are attributed to their source. OSB does not generate loss estimates or victim counts.
  • Corrections are published against the original reference number rather than edited silently.

08 Incident response

OSB's response team operates continuously. In the event of a personal data breach, OSB will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within the period required under the NDPA, and will notify affected individuals directly where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms. A public summary will be published on this page once the immediate response is complete.

09 Scope

Stated so that expectations are precise.

  • Infrastructure dependencies. Core infrastructure is operated by established third-party providers. Their certifications are theirs; OSB's controls are stated in section 03.
  • Emergency services. OSB's response team monitors live chat and inbound cases continuously and treats them as time-critical. OSB is not, and does not replace, a statutory emergency service. Where there is immediate risk to life or safety, contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country — and OSB will continue supporting you alongside them.
  • Jurisdiction. OSB is not a law enforcement agency and has no power of arrest, seizure or prosecution. Findings are analytical and are designed to support the authorities who do.
